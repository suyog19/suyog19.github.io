Support

Support the work.

I publish independent engineering research, practical experiments, open-source work, learning resources, and writing around software systems and AI-assisted engineering.

If that work is useful to you, you can help me keep more of it public. Support is entirely optional; the public material here does not become paywalled if you do not sponsor it.

Choose what fits

One-time support or ongoing sponsorship.

Both routes are handled by established payment providers. The site does not collect or store your card, UPI, or banking details.

One time

Support once

Use Razorpay for a one-time contribution in INR. You choose the amount on Razorpay before payment.

Monthly

Support regularly

Use GitHub Sponsors if you would like to provide ongoing monthly support for the public engineering work.

Sponsor on GitHub

GitHub opens in a new tab and handles sponsorship tiers, payment, and cancellation.

What it helps sustain

More useful work in public.

Engineering research and experiments. Exploring how AI-assisted software engineering, architecture, review, context, governance, and delivery practices are changing.

Open engineering resources. Reference implementations, repositories, design notes, and practical artifacts that make an idea inspectable rather than merely described.

Learning material. Explanations, short lessons, examples, and other resources that help software engineers develop durable understanding.

Independent writing. Software Signal Weekly and longer-form writing that connects research, tools, engineering practice, and real-world constraints.

No payment required

Other ways to help.

Attention, feedback, and useful recommendations matter too. These are genuinely helpful ways to support the work without spending anything.

Follow

Read Software Signal Weekly

Subscribe if the weekly synthesis is useful to you, and share an edition when it is useful to someone else.

 Use

Explore the open-source work

Use the repositories, star work you want to revisit, and raise useful issues or feedback where appropriate.

 Recommend

Tell someone who may benefit

A thoughtful recommendation to a learner, engineering team, reader, or collaborator is often more valuable than a broad repost.

A small note

Support is not a purchase.

Support helps sustain independent public work; it does not purchase consulting, training, priority support, or a guaranteed publication outcome. Those remain separate professional activities.

Payment processing is completed on Razorpay or GitHub, depending on the route you choose. Do not send payment details through the site's contact form.
