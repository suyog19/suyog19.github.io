How investigations work

From signal to a justified update.

  1. Observe and frame

    Separate a durable engineering question from short-lived tool noise.

  2. Gather and compare

    Use research, standards, practitioner evidence, existing engineering knowledge, and critical counter-evidence.

  3. Test where useful

    Use software experiments or prototypes when they can expose real behavior or constraints.

  4. Synthesise honestly

    Distinguish what appears supported, conditional, uncertain, contradicted, or still unexplored.

  5. Feed learning back

    Update the Framework, practical work, or confidence—not merely the publication queue.

Research becomes useful when it connects.

An investigation may lead to writing, an experiment, a reusable artifact, a Framework update, or a clearer question. None is manufactured simply to fill a category.
