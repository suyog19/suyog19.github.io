## Family review iterations

### Iteration 1 — Framework and Research

Research was accepted: the real active investigation, question, evidence posture,
method, and Framework/practical connections appear early in a distinct research-
index composition.

Framework received one Must-fix and one Should-fix:

- **Must:** its large masthead delayed the North Star and came too close to the
  homepage composition.
- **Should:** its mobile investigation loop became a tall word/arrow ladder.

### Iteration 2 — convergence and remaining families

Framework was compacted so the North Star enters the initial desktop viewport;
the mobile loop now wraps as a compact ordered sequence. Both findings were
resolved without changing canonical content.

Consulting, Learning, Website Services, Writing, Systems, and Weekly were accepted
without Must/Should findings. About received one Must-fix: remove a sentence that
asked visitors to reason about the retired equal-pillar/internal-architecture
model. It was replaced with positive visitor-facing language and accepted in a
focused convergence check.

The real beehiiv provider-loaded Weekly state was also verified without submission:
one iframe at the approved form URL, a labelled email input and Subscribe control,
and no page/console error. Consent, confirmation, privacy, unsubscribe, frequency,
and attention cost remained visible.

## Final Senior UX verdict

- **Must fix:** None.
- **Should fix:** None.
- **Optional:** Monitor Writing/Website Services page length and mobile Learning
  card density as content grows; these are maintenance cautions, not #609 defects.
- **Recommendation:** Strongly recommended; UX accepted.

### What works / preserve

- Framework optimizes comprehension and preserves the exact North Star, all eight
  canonical branches, cross-cutting Security, Methods of Investigation, and the
  evidence-feedback relationship. Branch numbers are semantic.
- Research optimizes investigation discovery and includes contradictory evidence,
  honest synthesis, testing, and feedback into practical work/Framework.
- Consulting exposes real advisory/review offers, scope, output, pricing, trust,
  boundaries, and low-pressure enquiry without outsourced-development language.
- Learning exposes practical professional learning, commitment, and starting-point
  selection while preserving scoped teal and learner functionality.
- Website Services remains adjacent and makes proposition, packages, ownership,
  process, cost, exclusions, and enquiry inspectable. Decorative package numbering
  was removed; ordered process numbering remains.
- Writing is an editorial discovery index; Systems is an inspectable artifact
  surface with explicit evidence boundaries.
- About makes Suyog visibly accountable and removes the stale three-pillar story.
- Weekly remains a calm subscription-decision page, not a competing brand.
- The family system inherits typography, Signal Red, neutral surfaces, rules,
  spacing, shell, links, focus, and responsive discipline without copying homepage
  composition. Dark panels remain exceptional.
