# Home page UX direction

- **Observed:** The home page is an orientation page, not a campaign landing
  page. Its hero establishes Suyog's domain and offers Training and Writing as
  the two immediate actions.
- **Observed:** The page then introduces the learning pathway, the Learn/Read/
  Explore choice, selected writing, selected systems, personal context, and a
  final contact invitation.
- **Observed:** Alternating white and pale surfaces, strong section spacing, and
  bordered card groups create rhythm without illustration-led decoration.
- **Derived principle:** Preserve the sequence from identity to choice to proof
  to personal context to contact. A new block must justify its place in that
  decision sequence.
- **Derived principle:** Featured content should demonstrate current substance,
  not turn the page into an exhaustive catalogue.

## Invariants

1. The opening promise remains the primary visual and semantic anchor.
2. Training, Writing, and Systems remain distinct, understandable routes.
3. Calls to action are few, specific, and visually ranked.
4. Suyog's experience supports the work without making the page résumé-first.
5. Mobile preserves the same narrative and action order.
