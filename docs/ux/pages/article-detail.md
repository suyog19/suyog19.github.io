# Article detail UX direction

- **Observed:** Article detail pages optimize for sustained reading: a clear
  title and metadata, a bounded reading column, serif headings, restrained body
  styling, and related reading after the article.
- **Observed:** Inline diagrams use simple native HTML/CSS patterns where useful;
  imagery supports explanation or publication context rather than filling space.
- **Previously decided:** The feedback widget appears after article content and
  before related or series navigation, with its stable article identifier and
  anonymous payload contract.
- **Derived principle:** Reading flow takes precedence over site promotion.

## Invariants

1. Title, topic/date context, and reading content have an obvious order.
2. Line length and vertical rhythm remain comfortable on desktop and mobile.
3. Links, lists, diagrams, and callouts remain semantically understandable.
4. Original-publication credit is explicit when applicable.
5. Feedback and related content do not interrupt the article body.
