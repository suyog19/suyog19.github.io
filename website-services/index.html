A normal engagement

The technical work is handled. The important decisions stay clear.

Purpose and structure

Short discovery, audience and objective clarification, content flow, headings, calls to action, and light refinement of customer-supplied copy.

Design and build

Practical UX, responsive implementation, reasonable performance, HTTPS deployment, basic accessibility, and deliberate use of dependencies or plugins.

Findability and contact

SEO fundamentals, analytics where included, and a sensible enquiry flow tested before launch.

Launch and handover

Final checks, deployment, ownership and cost notes, simple operating guidance, and limited support for post-launch defects.

Ownership without artificial lock-in

Your website should remain yours.

The default model is build-and-handover. You should be able to operate the commissioned website independently or continue with another provider. Customer-owned accounts are preferred for material services where practical, and Website Care remains optional.

Third-party costs stay visible

Domain, hosting or cloud, storage, email/SMS, APIs, payment gateways, premium themes/plugins, and SaaS costs are normally separate.

Customer owns the infrastructure. Software Signal manages it when needed. Meaningful recurring costs are discussed before implementation, without adding unnecessary complexity.

How the work moves

A clear next step, from first conversation to stable handover.

At each stage, you should understand what is needed from you, what happens next, and which decision you are being asked to make.

  1. 01Discover

    We learn about your work, audience, current situation, and goals.

  2. 02Define

    We agree the website's purpose, priorities, scope, and responsibilities.

  3. 03Structure

    We organise the content and journeys your visitors need to follow.

  4. 04Design

    We shape the visual direction and key page layouts for review.

  5. 05Build

    We turn the agreed direction into a responsive, working website.

  6. 06Review

    You review the website while we test and resolve launch issues.

  7. 07Launch

    We complete final checks, connect the approved site, and publish.

  8. 08Handover

    You receive ownership details and practical guidance for operating it.

  9. 09Stabilize

    We monitor the launch and fix covered post-launch defects.

Useful boundaries

A website service, not an open-ended software project.

Every site receives sensible engineering hygiene. Formal WCAG certification, penetration testing, guaranteed SEO rankings, guaranteed conversion, zero downtime, and other specialised assurance are separately scoped.

The standard offer does not initially include

  • Full e-commerce platforms or marketplaces
  • Membership platforms or complex custom booking systems
  • SaaS or product applications
  • Heavy custom backend or application development

About content: I can help with structure, page flow, headings, CTA wording, and light refinement. You remain responsible for authoritative facts, business and service details, prices, legal or compliance wording, and regulated claims. Full professional copywriting is separately scoped.
